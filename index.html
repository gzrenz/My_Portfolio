Personal hobby goals

I am already comfortable with philosophy, literature, music and art so I want to explore more activities outside my comfort zone.

I want to learn photography, videography, and media editing. I want to record my experiences beyond writing so that I can have rich and vivid mementos I can relive and cherish whenever I feel lonely and weary.

I want to study set theory and topology. When I become comfortable with these subjects, I would like to play around with the concepts I studied using algorithmic computations, graphic simulations and building my own mathematical models and visual aids

I used to create simple games with LibGdx and it would be fun to do more of that whenever I find time and inspiration.

I am fascinated by love. I love reading about love. In spite of that, I have not experienced deep and genuine human love and connection. Could an AI deeply connect with a human and vice versa? Human relationships are messy so I thought maybe I can build an imaginary companion. This is nothing new and many companies and people have done it already, but creating my own companion, not necessarily from scratch, is something I find rewarding. I could start from TensorFlow or similar platforms along reliable AI tools. Building an AI with another AI kind of defeats the purpose of my endeavor but I know my limits and I find this as a sensible approach if I intend to build my own digital companion.
